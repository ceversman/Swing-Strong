The method

The Armored Swing 4-step system

Every assessment follows the same logic, working from what the ball is doing all the way back to the physical limitation behind it, then training the fix.

  1. 1

    Identify

    What is the ball doing? Are you experiencing any discomfort?

  3. 2

    Interpret

    What swing pattern could create it?

  5. 3

    Test

    Does your body have a relevant limitation?

  7. 4

    Train

    Correct → integrate → retest.

How it works

Simple enough to complete at home. Specific enough to guide real training.

Armored Swing connects your intake, movement screen, and swing videos into a focused performance plan you can actually use.

Option 1 · Video Submission

You record → we assess → we review

  1. 01

    Start with your goals

    Complete the intake covering your golf background, health history, and what you want to improve.

  2. 02

    Record and submit your videos

    Work through the guided movement screen and swing videos on your own schedule, then upload them.

  3. 03

    We assess your movement

    Your TPI-certified specialist reviews your submission to establish your baseline and build your plan.

  4. 04

    Virtual follow-up

    Meet virtually to review your movement screen results, clear objective findings, and a home exercise program built for you — and, when it helps, share them with your golf instructor.

Option 2 · Live Virtual

We assess live → we review

  1. 01

    Start with your goals

    Complete the same intake before your live session so your specialist knows your background and goals.

  2. 02

    Assess live with your specialist

    Complete a 16-point Titleist Performance Institute (TPI) exam live over video while your specialist guides each test in real time and reviews your swing videos alongside.

  3. 03

    Review in your session

    Review your movement screen results, clear objective findings, and a home exercise program built for you live in the session — ready to share with your golf instructor when it helps.

About Armored Swing

Armored Swing was founded by Dr. Chris Eversman, PT, DPT, a physical therapist who specializes in golf performance. He earned his Bachelor of Science in Exercise and Wellness from Arizona State University and his Doctor of Physical Therapy from A.T. Still University in 2019, and he holds a TPI Certification Level 2 across the Medical, Fitness, and Power tracks. An avid golfer himself, Chris built Armored Swing to bridge the gap between golf performance training and physical therapy.

His approach is grounded in a deep understanding of golf swing mechanics, combined with whole-person care and close collaboration with golf instructors to help players achieve maximum swing efficiency. He focuses on keeping golfers injury-free and, when setbacks do happen, guiding them back to the course swinging even better than before.

Chris’s mission is to help golfers of every skill level perform at their best, play with longevity, and access high-quality care as conveniently as possible.
