How it works

Simple enough to complete at home. Specific enough to guide real training.

Swing Strong connects your intake, movement screen, and swing videos into a focused performance plan you can actually use.

About Swing Strong

Golf-specific physical therapy, built around how your body actually moves.

Swing Strong was founded by Dr. Chris Eversman, PT, DPT, a physical therapist who specializes in golf performance. He earned his Bachelor of Science in Exercise and Wellness from Arizona State University and his Doctor of Physical Therapy from A.T. Still University in 2019, and he holds a TPI Certification Level 2 across the Medical, Fitness, and Power tracks. An avid golfer himself, Chris built Swing Strong to bridge the gap between golf performance training and physical therapy.

His approach is grounded in a deep understanding of golf swing mechanics, combined with whole-person care and close collaboration with golf instructors to help players achieve maximum swing efficiency. He focuses on keeping golfers injury-free and, when setbacks do happen, guiding them back to the course swinging even better than before.

Chris’s mission is to help golfers of every skill level perform at their best, play with longevity, and access high-quality care as conveniently as possible.
