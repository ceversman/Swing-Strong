About Swing Strong

Golf-specific physical therapy, built around how your body actually moves.

Swing Strong was founded by Chris Eversman, PT, DPT, a Doctor of Physical Therapy and TPI certified provider who helps golfers connect what is happening in their bodies to what is happening in their swings. The goal is simple: find the movement limitations that quietly hold back your comfort, consistency, and power, then give you a clear, practical plan to work on them.

With TPI Level 1 and Level 2 certifications across the Medical, Fitness, and Power tracks, Chris blends a golf and health intake, a golf-specific movement screen, and optional swing video review into individualized findings you can use on your own or share with your instructor. It is fully remote, so you can complete it from home and meet virtually to walk through your results.

  • Doctor of Physical Therapy PT, DPT — clinical background in movement and rehabilitation
  • TPI Level 1 & Level 2 Certified across the Medical, Fitness, and Power tracks
  • 100% remote Complete from home with a virtual follow-up
